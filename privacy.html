Privacy policy

Numbers Center is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the channels it reads. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's consent, it uses the read-only Google scopes youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly to read the list of the channel's videos and their statistics. It does not access viewers' personal data, and it does not upload, change or delete anything.

Where the data goes

Statistics are stored on the owner's computer and in the owner's private backup repository on GitHub. The owner also views them on a private page in their Claude account and may ask the Claude assistant to analyse them. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Access tokens are kept only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer.

How to revoke access and delete data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app has a command that deletes all stored data of a channel.

Google and YouTube terms

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Коротко по-русски

Приложением пользуется только владелец каналов. Оно читает статистику его каналов и хранит её на его компьютере, в его приватной копии на GitHub и на его личной странице в Claude. Цифры не продаются и никому больше не передаются. Отозвать доступ можно на странице myaccount.google.com/permissions.
